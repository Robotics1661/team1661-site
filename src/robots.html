2025 · REEFSCAPE

2025 robot

Built for Reefscape. An elevator and a wrist let it score coral at different heights, and a climber handles the endgame. It runs on swerve, and a Limelight camera helps it drive up and line up on the reef automatically.

2024 · CRESCENDO

Eclipse

Eclipse, in our pit. You can see our sponsors’ logos on the polycarbonate panels. The orange rollers pull game pieces in through the intake. It won us the Excellence in Engineering Award at the Los Angeles Regional.

Our process

How we build a robot

  1. 01

    Plan

    Read the new game, then decide what the robot should be great at.

  2. 02

    Design

    CAD and prototypes, before we cut any real parts.

  3. 03

    Build

    Machining, assembly and wiring in the robotics room.

  4. 04

    Program

    Autonomous and driver controls. The code is on GitHub.

  5. 05

    Compete

    Fix what broke between matches, and go again.
