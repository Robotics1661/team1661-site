Who we are

About the team

Team 1661 is a FIRST Robotics Competition team located at The Buckley School in Sherman Oaks, CA. We’ve been competing in FRC since our team’s inception in 2005 and have won multiple competition awards over the years.

Our team typically consists of 15–30 members, and we are working to expand both the team and our efforts to promote STEAM education in our school and local community.

We start working in the summer for the spring competition. The team has four areas: Manufacturing, Media, CAD and Programming.
