172|About the team
173|Team 1661 is a FIRST Robotics Competition team located at <1>The Buckley School</1> in Sherman Oaks, CA. We’ve been competing in FRC since our team’s inception in 2005 and have won multiple competition awards over the years.
174|Our team typically consists of 15–30 members, and we are working to expand both the team and our efforts to promote STEAM education in our school and local community.
175|We start working in the summer for the spring competition. The team has four areas: Manufacturing, Media, CAD and Programming.
